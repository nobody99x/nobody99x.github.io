Privacy & Transparency Commitment

We believe browser extensions should respect your workspace and personal boundaries above all else. Across all published tools, we uphold the following strict principles:

  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, monetize, or broker user information to advertisers or brokers.
  • Client-Side Execution: All core functions run locally inside your browser; no files or media are stored externally.
  • Minimal Permissions: Only the exact permissions necessary for operational performance are requested.
  • No Hidden Telemetry: We do not track personal browsing habits or keystrokes.

For individual extension policies or compliance questions, please contact our support desk.
